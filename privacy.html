Privacy Policy

AAI Agency Instagram Bot  ·  Last updated: June 12, 2026

1. Introduction

AAI Agency ("we", "our", "us") operates an Instagram automation tool ("the App") that automatically responds to Instagram comments and sends direct messages on behalf of the account owner. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

When a user comments on an Instagram post where our automation is active, we collect and store:

  • Instagram username
  • Instagram User ID (provided by Meta platform)
  • Comment text
  • Date and time of the comment

We do not collect passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal information beyond what Instagram's API provides.

3. How We Use the Data

Collected data is used solely to:

  • Trigger automated replies to comments based on keywords
  • Send automated direct messages to users who commented
  • Maintain a log of leads/interactions for the account owner

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.

4. Meta Platform Data

This App uses the Instagram Graph API and complies with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies. We only request permissions necessary for the core functionality: reading comments and sending messages on behalf of the connected Instagram Business account.

5. Data Retention

Interaction logs (username, comment, date) are retained for as long as the account owner uses the service. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

6. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please contact us at the email below. We will remove your information from our systems within 30 days of the request.

7. Security

All data is stored on a secured server. Access tokens are encrypted and never exposed publicly. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.
